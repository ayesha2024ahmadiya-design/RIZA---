About Us

RIZA Foundation is an online Islamic education institute. We teach authentic knowledge with patience, good manners and regular follow-up, so every student grows in faith and character.

Our mission

To make sound Islamic education easy to reach for every family, wherever they live.

Our approach

Small classes, kind teachers, clear lessons and honest weekly feedback for parents.

Our promise

A safe, respectful learning space where students feel confident to ask and to grow.

All classes and Sunday Dars take place on Google Meet. Google Chat is used for announcements and student communication.
